How It Works

1v1 Peer-to-Peer Battles

Users make sports predictions and are automatically matched with an opponent. The user who scores more points wins the pot. BattlePicks facilitates the match and takes a 10% rake — we never play against our users.

STEP 01

Choose Entry

User picks an entry amount — $5, $25 or $50. Real money, one currency, no coins or promotional balances.

STEP 02

Make Prediction

User builds a roster of three player props — one pick per player, across at least two teams. Riskier picks earn more points.

STEP 03

Auto-Match

System matches the roster against another user at the same entry level. No more than one player may overlap between the two rosters, and neither user sees the other’s picks before the reveal.

STEP 04

Settlement

Games finish, picks are scored by risk-based points. Higher total points wins. Winner receives the pot minus the 10% platform rake.

Scoring System

Battle Points

Every pick carries a Battle Points value derived from how hard it is to hit. Harder picks are worth more. Users balance difficulty against reward to outscore their opponent.

Key Facts

Platform Overview

Revenue Model

10% rake on every completed match. Platform is a neutral facilitator — it never takes a position against a user and never sets a line.

Market Data

Player lines come from The Odds API, a third-party market-data aggregator. BattlePicks does not originate, set or modify any line — it consumes published market data and nothing else.

Matching

Fully automated. Users cannot choose opponents. Entries are matched by format and entry amount, and no more than one player may overlap between two matched rosters — the rule that keeps every contest an independent test of two rosters.

Supported Sports

NFL is live, with player props across the full slate. Additional sports follow as data coverage and demand allow.

Deposit Limits

$500 deposit cap per rolling 24 hours. Withdrawals are ACH only, $25 minimum, two per rolling 24 hours. Conservative limits to minimize fraud risk and chargebacks.

Entity

BattlePicks LLC. Registered in Texas (July 2025). Single-member LLC. Operating from California.

Compliance

Trust & Safety

✓

State-Level Geofencing

Every real-money action is checked server-side against a per-state eligibility list before it executes, and every check is logged. A failed or unavailable check blocks the action. Blocked states see no real-money options.

✓

Age Verification

18+ for real-money play, 19+ in Nebraska. States with a 21+ minimum are not served. Enforced at registration and again at identity verification.

✓

Anti-Collusion

All matching is random and automated. No friend challenges. No opponent selection. One account per person with email and phone verification.

✓

AML / KYC Controls

Document identity verification before any withdrawal. One account per person, with email and phone verification. Velocity limits and transaction monitoring for suspicious patterns.
